If delivery goes wrong

  • Lost in transit. We will replace or refund at your election. We deal with the carrier, not you.
  • Damaged in transit. Tell us within {{TRANSIT_DAYS}} days. Keep the packaging. Replacement or refund at your election.
  • Significantly late. Where delivery takes materially longer than the timeframe we gave you, that may be a failure of the consumer guarantee to supply within a reasonable time. Your rights are set out in returns and refunds.
  • Wrong address supplied. If the address you gave us is wrong and the parcel is returned, we will resend it once you pay the reshipping cost, or refund the goods less the original shipping.

Incorrect or incomplete orders

If we send the wrong item or miss part of your order, tell us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} and we will correct it at our cost, including return postage. This is our error, not yours, and you should never be out of pocket for it.
