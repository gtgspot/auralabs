Questions

Clear limits are part of the answer.

The current page is a pre-launch presentation. It does not fill missing particulars with assumptions.

What does GHK-Cu mean here?

GHK-Cu refers to the copper complex of the tripeptide GHK. On the cosmetic ingredient presentation used here, it is named Copper Tripeptide-1. This naming does not itself establish a product claim.

Does the blue colour have a calming health effect?

No such claim is made. The blue is a product-derived visual cue in the brand system; it is not represented as lowering stress, blood pressure or producing any other health effect.

Is AuraLabs making therapeutic claims?

No therapeutic effect is claimed on this page. The final product classification, permitted claims and Australian supply pathway must be verified before the product is offered.

Where is the full ingredient list?

The final INCI list has not been supplied for publication on this page. It must appear, together with directions, warnings, net contents and storage information, before sale.

When will orders open?

Only after the formulation, product status, responsible entity, ABN, contact details, pricing, delivery settings and returns process are completed and checked. No launch date is represented here.
